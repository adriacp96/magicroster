Install Magic Roster

Install this tool to quickly access your roster data from any supported page.

Magic Roster

💻 On Computer:

  • Click and drag the Magic Roster button up to your browser's Bookmarks Bar.

📱 On Phone / Tablet:

  • Step 1: Tap the button above to copy the code.
  • Step 2: Bookmark this current page.
  • Step 3: Edit the bookmark you just created. Change its name to "Magic Roster" and paste the copied code into the "URL" or "Address" field.
